768px catalog PNGs. These files are bundled locally and loaded near the viewport
with `ProgressiveAssetImage`, so rendering does not depend on third-party image
requests or hotlink permission. No imagery was AI-generated or retouched.

https://printernoble.com/product/ and https://printernoble.com/product/page/2/.
Four images total approximately 1.06 MiB; the larger originals total 2.5 MiB.

Use descriptive product names: the thermal and dot-matrix pages have differences
between URL/model labels and specification series names. Attendance copy refers
to the pictured punch-card machine, not facial recognition. Power banks replace
the previous binding-equipment card because no matching binding product image
was found in the official two-page catalog.
